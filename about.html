Our story

Closing the gap between creativity and quality

Our founders noticed a wide gap between creatively meeting clients’ construction needs and achieving high levels of quality. Towards the end of 2022, Miedema Africa Limited was born with a youthful, resilient and innovative team focused on mentorship and excellence.

Today we deliver project management, general construction and civil works for residential, commercial and industrial clients.
